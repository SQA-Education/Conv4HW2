3 · Write A Casual Invitation

Imagine you run into a coworker or neighbor who has been very friendly lately. You want to invite them to do something small and casual next week to build a real friendship. Write a short 6–8 sentence dialogue that moves from a basic hallway greeting into a specific invitation — and gives them an easy way to say no.

💬 Include a Concrete Proposal + a Scheduling Softener:
State a specific day, time, and casual activity, then immediately soften it so they can decline comfortably.
Example: “Do you want to grab coffee Thursday around 4? No pressure at all if you're slammed — we can always play it by ear.”

📝 Use at least two words: touch base, grab coffee, slammed, take a rain check, play it by ear, low-key, up for.
